# FinSave AI design system

## Direction

Operate-mode financial workspace: a quiet light canvas framed by an opaque navy rail, with digital green used only for active navigation, actions, charts, and progress.

## Foundations

- Canvas: `#F7F8FC`; surfaces: white; ink: `#151A2D`; muted: `#74809A`.
- Brand green: `#70C95E` (bright `#82D671`, deep `#5EB34D`); navy rail: `#101827`.
- Typography: Inter UI sans with tabular figures. Headings are compact, weight-led, and never rely on decorative display treatments.
- Rhythm: 8px baseline; page padding `p-6`/`p-8`; standard gaps `gap-6`; surface radius 16px.
- Elevation: a single diffuse `0 8px 30px rgba(0,0,0,.02)` shadow on white surfaces.

## Signature element

Credit health is shown as a precise green-led score scale and factor breakdown, rather than a generic marketing gauge.

## Responsive rules

The rail becomes a modal drawer below `lg`; header metadata reduces before controls; grids collapse without hiding finance labels or navigation links.
