# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individuals and MSME owners in Rwanda managing several financial sources, including bank and mobile-money accounts. Lender staff use a structurally isolated portal for consented business scores.

## Product Purpose

FinSave AI brings spending activity, savings goals, explainable MSME credit scoring, and insurance matching into one financial coaching workspace.

## Positioning

It combines multiple linked bank and MoMo accounts into a single, transparent financial view while keeping credit-score access consent-gated.

## Operating Context

Users inspect transaction activity, budgets, goals, and credit standing on desktop and mobile; figures are presented in RWF and may be filtered by account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, shadcn-style UI components, and Recharts. Currency is held in integer minor units or Decimal, never raw floats. Consumer data is scoped to verified sessions. The existing route content and navigation text must be retained through the redesign.

## Brand Commitments

The name is FinSave AI. The uploaded `designs/insipiration.png` is the binding visual reference for the application shell: opaque dark-navy navigation, a light neutral workspace, premium digital violet, high-clarity finance data, and generous 8px-based spacing.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing product routes, authenticated UI, and the dashboard visual reference at `designs/insipiration.png`. Dashboard values are live product data, not marketing claims.

## Product Principles

- Make financial information calm, legible, and explicit.
- Keep account sources visible within aggregate financial views.
- Treat user privacy and lender consent as first-class product behavior.
- Favor practical coaching actions over decorative finance metaphors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must be responsive to mobile, retain visible keyboard focus, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
